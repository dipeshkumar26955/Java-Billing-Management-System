# Java Billing Management System

## 📌 About the Project
The **Java Billing Management System** is a console-based application developed using Java. The main purpose of this project is to manage products, customers, billing, payments, and inventory efficiently.

This project is being developed step by step using Object-Oriented Programming (OOP), file handling, and Java collections.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Manage product and customer information.
- Generate bills and invoices automatically.
- Calculate discounts, GST, and final payable amounts.
- Maintain inventory and stock records.
- Store and retrieve data using files.
- Maintain sales history and generate reports.

## ✨ Planned Features

### 1. Product Management
- Add new products.
- Display all products.
- Store product details in files.
- Load saved product data.
- Update and delete products.
- Search products by ID or name.

### 2. Customer Management
- Add new customers.
- Display all customers.
- Store customer details in files.
- Load saved customer data.
- Update and delete customer records.
- Search customers by ID or name.

### 3. Billing Management
- Create a new bill for a customer.
- Add multiple products to a bill.
- Enter product quantities.
- Calculate subtotal and total amount.
- Validate product availability and quantity.

### 4. Discount Management
- Apply discounts to eligible bills.
- Calculate discount amounts automatically.
- Display the final amount after discount.

### 5. GST / Tax Management
- Calculate GST on purchased products.
- Add tax to the bill.
- Display subtotal, tax, discount, and final amount separately.

### 6. Invoice Generation
- Generate a unique bill or invoice number.
- Display customer and product details.
- Show quantities, prices, GST, discounts, and total amount.
- Save invoice details for future reference.
- Provide a printable invoice format.

### 7. Inventory and Stock Management
- Maintain available product quantities.
- Reduce stock after a successful sale.
- Prevent sales when stock is insufficient.
- Display low-stock products.
- Update stock when new products arrive.

### 8. Payment Management
- Record payment details.
- Support cash, UPI, and other payment methods as simulated payment records.
- Track paid and pending amounts.
- Display payment status.

### 9. Sales History
- Save completed sales records.
- View previous bills.
- Search sales by invoice number or customer.
- Display sales history by date.

### 10. Reports and Analytics
- Calculate total sales.
- Display daily and overall sales summaries.
- Identify frequently sold products.
- Show low-stock products.
- Generate basic business reports.

### 11. Smart Product Recommendations
- Recommend products based on previous sales or frequently purchased items.
- Display popular products to help users make purchasing decisions.

*This feature will initially use simple rule-based logic; advanced recommendation techniques may be added later.*

### 12. File Handling and Data Management
- Save product, customer, billing, and sales information in files.
- Load saved records when the application starts.
- Keep records organized in separate files.
- Handle missing files and invalid data appropriately.

### 13. User-Friendly Menu
- Provide a menu-driven console interface.
- Validate user input.
- Display clear success and error messages.
- Allow users to navigate between different modules.
